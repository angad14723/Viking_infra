Products

The geosynthetic materials we supply, each matched to an application and to the specification your project is assessed against. Data sheets available on request.

Geogrids

Uniaxial geogrids carry load in one direction for reinforced soil walls and slopes. Biaxial geogrids spread load both ways for pavement base reinforcement and ground stabilisation. Available in polyester, polypropylene and HDPE, in a range of tensile strengths.

  • Reinforced soil walls & steepened slopes
  • Pavement base and subgrade reinforcement
  • Basal reinforcement over soft ground

Geocells

Expandable HDPE honeycomb panels that confine infill — soil, aggregate or concrete — to form a stiff, erosion-resistant mattress. Used for slope protection, load support over weak soils and channel lining. Textured and perforated grades available.

  • Slope and channel erosion protection
  • Load support for roads and platforms
  • Gravity retaining faces

Polymeric Reinforcement Strips

High-tenacity polyester cores in a polyethylene sheath, used as soil reinforcement in reinforced soil walls with precast concrete panel facing. Low creep, high durability and consistent connection strength.

  • Precast-panel reinforced soil walls
  • Bridge abutments and high walls
  • Connection accessories and fixings

Erosion Control Blankets & Mats

Biodegradable coir and straw blankets protect newly graded slopes while vegetation establishes, then break down naturally. Permanent turf reinforcement mats give long-term root anchorage on steep or high-flow surfaces.

  • Coir blankets (various GSM)
  • Straw / coir composite blankets
  • Permanent turf reinforcement mats

Gabions & Wire Mesh Systems

Double-twist hexagonal woven mesh in PVC or Zn-Al coating, fabricated into gabion boxes, mattresses and sack gabions for retaining structures, channel protection and scour control.

  • Gabion boxes and reno mattresses
  • Rockfall and slope-drape netting
  • Lacing wire, clips and geotextile filters

Rockfall Protection Systems

High-tensile steel wire mesh, cable nets and anchor components for draped and pinned rockfall systems on hill roads and cut slopes. Specified to slope geometry and expected block energy.

  • High-tensile drape and anchored mesh
  • Cable nets and spike plates
  • Anchors, ropes and barrier components

GFRP / FRP Rebars

Glass-fibre reinforced polymer bars — non-corroding, non-conductive and roughly a quarter of the weight of steel. Used where corrosion, magnetic neutrality or long maintenance-free life matter: decks, approach slabs, marine and drainage structures. To IRC:137-2022.

  • Straight bars, stirrups and bent shapes
  • Marine, coastal and de-icing-salt environments
  • Non-magnetic and electrically insulating structures

Geotextiles & Geocomposites

Needle-punched nonwoven geotextiles for separation, filtration and protection, and drainage geocomposites that combine a core with filter fabric to carry water while retaining soil.

  • Nonwoven separation & filtration fabrics
  • Drainage geocomposites and fin drains
  • Protection layers for membranes and liners

Soil Nails & Anchors

Solid and hollow bar systems with couplers, plates and grout accessories for slope stabilisation, excavation support and mesh-faced soil-nail walls.

  • Solid bar and self-drilling hollow bar
  • Bearing plates, nuts and centralisers
  • Facing mesh and shotcrete accessories
