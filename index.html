Solutions

Engineering applications we support

Six problem areas where geosynthetics deliver a better result than conventional methods.

Retaining & Reinforced Soil

Reinforced soil walls and steepened slopes with modular facing — taller, faster and lower-cost than RCC retaining walls.

Slope Protection & Erosion Control

Vegetated geocells and coir blankets that hold embankment and hill-cut slopes while turf cover establishes.

Ground Improvement

Basal reinforcement, load-transfer platforms and subgrade stabilisation over soft and variable soils.

Rockfall & Geohazard Mitigation

Draped and anchored wire-mesh systems, barriers and soil nailing for unstable rock and soil slopes.

Pavement Reinforcement

Biaxial geogrids in the base and subgrade to cut aggregate thickness and extend pavement life.

Drainage, Filtration & Separation

Nonwoven geotextiles and geocomposite drains for subsurface drainage and separation over weak formations.

Products

The geosynthetic range

Materials we supply, matched to the application and the specification.

Geogrids

Uniaxial for walls and slopes; biaxial for pavements and ground stabilisation.

Geocells

HDPE honeycomb confinement for slope protection, load support and channel lining.

Polymeric Reinforcement Strips

High-tenacity geostrips for reinforced soil walls with precast panel facing.

Erosion Control Blankets

Biodegradable coir and straw blankets, and permanent turf reinforcement mats.

Gabions & Wire Mesh

PVC / Zn-Al coated double-twist gabion boxes, mattresses and rockfall netting.

GFRP Rebars

Corrosion-proof glass-fibre reinforcement, about a quarter of the weight of steel.
