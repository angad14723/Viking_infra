Solutions

Six problem areas where an engineered geosynthetic system is faster to build, longer lasting and lower in whole-life cost than conventional construction.

Retaining & Reinforced Soil Structures

Layers of soil reinforcement (geogrid or polymeric strips) tied to a modular facing let you build near-vertical retaining structures and steepened slopes without heavy RCC. Ideal for road widening, embankment ends, bridge approaches and level-difference sites.

  • Reinforced soil walls with precast or block facing
  • Reinforced soil slopes and steepened embankments
  • Bridge abutments and approach ramps
  • Design sections, drawings and stability checks

Slope Protection & Erosion Control

Confinement and cover systems that keep soil in place on cut and fill slopes until vegetation takes over. Ranges from biodegradable blankets for gentle slopes to infilled geocells for steep or high-flow surfaces.

  • HDPE geocells with soil, aggregate or concrete infill
  • Coir and straw erosion-control blankets
  • Permanent turf reinforcement mats
  • Channel and drain lining

Ground Improvement & Stabilisation

Reinforcement placed at the base of embankments and working platforms spreads load, controls differential settlement and lets construction proceed over soft or variable ground.

  • Basal reinforcement over soft soils
  • Load-transfer platforms over piles and columns
  • Subgrade stabilisation and separation
  • Working-platform design for plant loads

Rockfall & Geohazard Mitigation

Wire-mesh and anchor systems that contain loose rock and stabilise soil slopes along hill roads and cuttings — designed for the slope geometry and expected block sizes.

  • Draped (simple) and anchored (pinned) mesh systems
  • High-tensile steel mesh and cable nets
  • Soil nailing with shotcrete or mesh facing
  • Rockfall barrier components

Pavement Reinforcement

Biaxial geogrid placed within the granular layer interlocks with aggregate to form a stiffer, more stable base — reducing layer thickness, controlling rutting and extending pavement life over weak subgrades.

  • Base-course reinforcement and stabilisation
  • Subgrade improvement over soft formations
  • Haul roads, yards and working platforms
  • Section optimisation against design traffic

Drainage, Filtration & Separation

Nonwoven geotextiles and drainage geocomposites keep fill and subgrade apart, let water through while holding fines, and carry seepage away from structures and pavements.

  • Separation over soft subgrades
  • Filtration behind walls and around drains
  • Geocomposite drains for subsurface water
  • Erosion filters under rock protection
