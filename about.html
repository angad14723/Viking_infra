About Vikings Infra Solutions

A geosynthetics and ground-engineering company helping infrastructure projects build stronger ground with lighter, faster, longer-lasting systems.

Who We Are

Specification-led geosynthetic solutions

[Replace this paragraph with your company background.] Vikings Infra Solutions Private Limited works with contractors, consultants and project owners to select, supply and support engineered geosynthetic systems — reinforced soil structures, slope protection, ground improvement, pavement reinforcement and rockfall mitigation.

Our focus is getting the engineering right first: the correct product for the soil, the loading and the specification, backed by drawings, calculations and installation support so the system performs for its full design life.

Mission

To make durable, sustainable ground-engineering solutions the default choice on Indian infrastructure — well designed, correctly installed and fairly priced.

Vision

To be a trusted geosynthetics partner known for engineering depth, honest advice and dependable delivery.

Values

The right solution over the easy sell, materials that meet the spec, and support that continues through installation.

How We Work

From specification to site

1. Understand

Review drawings, soil data, loading and the governing specification.

2. Design

Propose a system with sections, drawings and stability calculations to IRC / MoRTH / IS.

3. Supply

Deliver products with test certificates, scheduled to the construction programme.

4. Support

Method statements, installation guidance and site supervision through execution.
